Prepare your system

Frequently asked questions

Does the browser upload my configuration or secrets?

No. Validation, sizing, secret generation, checksums and ZIP creation run locally in this browser. The site has no analytics or configuration API.

Is the recommendation a capacity guarantee?

No. The report separates minimum resources from a recommendation with at least 30% planning headroom. Production still requires representative load and recovery testing.

Why does Kubernetes remain on oCIS 7.1.4?

The official chart 0.7.0 declares app version 7.1.4. The generator preserves that version boundary under open issue #6 instead of silently presenting it as an 8.2 deployment.
